ACME

Open-source app that runs every instrument in a lab setup from one window: mass flow controllers, RH probes, chillers, O₂ meters and valves. It logs everything to CSV with live plots, and runs scripted multi-day experiments unattended with built-in safety stops.

Python · PyQt6 · MIT licence
